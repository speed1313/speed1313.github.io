\cvsection{Research Interests}

{\small
My research focuses on building \textbf{data resources}, namely training datasets
and benchmarks, and on using them to develop \textbf{vision-language models (VLMs)}
and to \textbf{benchmark LLM agents} on economically valuable, long-horizon tasks
beyond coding and math.
}
