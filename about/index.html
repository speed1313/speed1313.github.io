Research

My research focuses on building the resources that drive progress in AI, and on using them to develop and evaluate models:

  • constructing large-scale training datasets and reliable benchmarks (e.g., WAON, Jagle, JAMMEval, HakushoBench)
  • developing vision-language models with these resources (e.g., LLM-jp-4-VL 9B)
  • benchmarking LLM agents on economically valuable, long-horizon tasks (e.g., CoffeeBench, EDINET-Bench)
